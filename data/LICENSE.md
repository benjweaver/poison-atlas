# Data licence

The species files in `data/species/` (names, summaries, toxicity, symptom,
lookalike, and habitat notes, ranges and their cited reasons) are © 2026 Ben
Weaver and licensed under [Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

## How to credit

If you use or adapt this data, credit it where people will see it (on the
page, in the app's about screen, or alongside the map), for example:

> Species data from Poison Atlas by Ben Weaver
> (https://github.com/benjweaver/poison-atlas), CC BY-SA 4.0

and share your changes to the data under the same licence.

## Other sources

Material from elsewhere keeps its own terms:

- **Photos** (`data/images.json`) are Wikimedia Commons files, each under the
  licence and author credit recorded beside it.
- **Record counts and dots** (`data/gbif.json`, `public/occurrence/`) are
  derived from occurrence data served by [GBIF.org](https://www.gbif.org/),
  published by many institutions under CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-NC.
- **Native and introduced status for plants** comes from the World Checklist
  of Vascular Plants (WCVP), published by the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, and
  served through GBIF.
- **Boundaries, land, rivers, and lakes** (`public/geo/`, `data/land.json`)
  come from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) and are public
  domain.
- **Quotes** in cited reasons are short excerpts from the sources they cite.
